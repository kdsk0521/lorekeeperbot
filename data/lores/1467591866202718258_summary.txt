[Project: Suture City] Lorebook v4.0 - Core Edition

Creation Intent: A fusion of Post-Apocalypse + Steampunk + Occult. In a steam civilization that burns souls and memories as fuel, survivors armed with body modifications and cold weapons struggle to live another day.
Core Theme: "Soulification." A new industrial age where souls and memories have replaced coal. Curses are the flip side of blessings, and a lead-gray city where automatons and humans coexist.
